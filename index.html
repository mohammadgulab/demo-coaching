FAQ

Questions parents ask us

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp, we reply the same day.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Sit in any batch for one day, no fee and no form. Just tell us the day.

How many students are in one batch?

Never more than 30, so every student gets asked and every copy gets checked.

Can I pay the fee every month?

Yes. Monthly, or every three months with a small discount. UPI, card or cash at the office.

What if my child misses a class?

Notes are kept for them, and the doubt desk covers the missed topic the next day.

Do you teach in Hindi or English?

Both. Notes are in English, and teachers explain in Hindi whenever it helps.
